ChemForge

2D is the editing view: build and rearrange structures flat, including isomers. 3D relaxes the same structure into its VSEPR shape; drag empty space to orbit, pinch or wheel to zoom, two fingers to pan. Press X, Y or Z (or the axis buttons) to look straight down that axis; press again for the opposite side.

Drag an atom onto another to bond with the selected order (Single, Double, Triple), or tap a bond to cycle its order and remove it. You can edit in both views. Drag from the tray onto the canvas to add atoms. Erase is a brush: press and drag across atoms or bonds to delete them in one stroke (one Undo restores the whole stroke). Long-press an atom to change its charge. Pin freezes the selected molecule (or all) while you build.

Bond energy: pin a molecule, then drag one atom away. The bond is modelled with a Morse potential; the energy you supply is shown over the stretched bond against its dissociation energy (kJ/mol). When it reaches that value the bond breaks homolytically and both fragments become radicals. Values are textbook mean bond energies; pairs not in the table are estimated with Pauling's electronegativity relation and marked est. Gas-phase model, not solvent.

Hand mode (installed app or hosted page): turns on the selfie camera and tracks up to two hands. Steer the ring cursor, pinch thumb and index to grab, open your fingers to release. Pull an element tile onto the canvas, or quickly pinch-tap a tile to arm it: then pinch empty space to place it, or an atom to bond a new one to it (pinch the tile again to stop). Pinch an atom and drop it on another to bond. Pinch empty space to pan (2D) or orbit (3D); pinch with both hands on empty space to zoom. Hold one atom with each hand and pull apart to stretch the bond between them until it breaks. Pinch a toolbar button to press it.

Aromatic ring templates are in the Rings tab. Transition and f-block metals are approximate and cannot be logged. Names come only from the offline Known table.
